# Billing support

Category: billing
Version: 1.0

Explain invoice line items using the order and billing records visible to the authorized support employee. Do not infer a charge from memory or from a different customer's account.

Support staff must not promise a refund, credit, or charge reversal based only on an AI-generated suggestion. Gather the transaction reference available in the internal record and route disputed charges to billing specialists.

Never place full card numbers, security codes, bank passwords, or authentication secrets in a ticket or an assistant prompt.
