# Escalation procedure

Category: escalation
Version: 1.0

Escalate suspected account compromise, failed identity verification, a security-sensitive request, an unresolved delivery investigation, or a case outside the support employee's authorization.

Include the ticket ID, observed facts, relevant tool results, steps already attempted, and the reason for escalation. Exclude passwords, tokens, one-time codes, full payment credentials, and unrelated customer information.

If information is missing, state what is missing and ask a focused follow-up question. Never make a sensitive state change to avoid an escalation.
