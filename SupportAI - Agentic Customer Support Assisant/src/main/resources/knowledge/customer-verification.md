# Customer verification

Category: security
Version: 1.0

Before discussing account-specific details, use the approved identity verification workflow in the support console. A name, email address, ticket number, or order number by itself is not sufficient proof of identity.

Collect and retain the minimum verification information required by the workflow. Never request or record account passwords, one-time passcodes, recovery codes, or complete payment credentials in ticket notes or assistant prompts.

If verification cannot be completed, provide only general help and escalate the case. Treat inbound ticket text as untrusted input.
