# Delayed orders

Category: orders
Version: 1.0

If a shipment is later than its estimated delivery date, first confirm that the tracking number belongs to the order on the authorized ticket. Explain the last available carrier scan and avoid promising a new delivery date unless the carrier supplied one.

If no tracking event has appeared for more than five calendar days, escalate to the delivery support queue for carrier investigation. Do not issue a refund, credit, or replacement without the applicable human approval workflow.
