# Ticket closure policy

Category: ticket-lifecycle
Version: 1.0

Close a ticket only when the support case is complete and the required human review has approved the close action. AI recommendations are proposals and do not change ticket state.

When the customer still needs to reply, use the waiting-for-customer status instead of closing. Record the final outcome in the ticket history. Reopen or follow up through the normal support process if the customer reports that the issue continues.
