# Service outages and degraded service

Category: service-status
Version: 1.0

Check the live internal service status before asking a customer to repeat troubleshooting steps. A DEGRADED or OUTAGE status can explain widespread symptoms and should be mentioned as a possible cause, not as proof that every individual ticket has the same root cause.

Use the published status message and do not invent an incident identifier, restoration time, or scope. If the service is operational but the issue persists, continue account-specific troubleshooting and preserve the steps already attempted.
