# Order delivery policy

Category: orders
Version: 1.0

Check the order status and carrier tracking before advising a customer. The estimated delivery date is an estimate, not a guarantee. Share only the tracking information associated with the order on the authorized ticket.

For a shipped order that is past its estimate, explain the latest carrier event and the published estimate. Do not mark an order delivered, cancel it, promise compensation, or change a delivery address from an AI recommendation.

When tracking has not changed for more than five calendar days, follow the delayed-order procedure and escalate when its conditions are met.
