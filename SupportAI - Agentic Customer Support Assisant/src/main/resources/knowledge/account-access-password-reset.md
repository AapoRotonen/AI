# Account access after a password reset

Category: account-access
Version: 1.0

Ask the customer to retry sign-in with the new password after confirming that the reset completed successfully. Check the authentication service status before requesting another reset. Repeated resets do not fix an active service incident and may trigger additional verification.

If sign-in still fails while authentication is operational, verify the account identifier using the customer verification procedure. Never ask a customer to send a password, one-time code, recovery code, or full payment details to support.

Record the steps already attempted in the ticket. Escalate suspected account compromise or repeated lockouts to senior support. Do not promise a resolution time that is not published in the current service status.
